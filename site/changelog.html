0.2.0 23 September 2026 current

The first public release. Everything below is what DATUM can do as of this version.

  • Modelling. History-based solids on OpenCASCADE: extrude, revolve, sweep, loft, fillet, chamfer, shell, holes, rectangular and circular patterns, mirror and booleans. Features stay editable, and edges keep their fillets when dimensions upstream change.
  • Sketching. Lines, arcs, circles, splines, rectangles, fillet, trim and offset, with coincident, horizontal, vertical, parallel, perpendicular, tangent, equal, concentric, collinear, midpoint, point-on and symmetric constraints. Dimensions can be named and referenced by other dimensions, and accept expressions like (10-2+d2)/2.
  • Assemblies. Place parts by reference rather than by copy, constrain them mate or flush with an offset, and see which way a face points before you pick the second one. Editing a part in its own tab updates the assembly.
  • Drawings. Base, projected, section, detail and auxiliary views with hidden line removal. Sections hatch their cut faces from the model's material. Parts lists read the assembly and balloons take their numbers from the list. Dimensions snap to model vertices and follow the model when it changes. True vector PDF, SVG, DXF and print.
  • 2D CAM. Nest flat parts on a sheet, offset toolpaths for the cutter, add leads and dogbone corner relief, and export DXF.
  • Projects. An Inventor-style project file per folder, with its own recent files and its own drawing templates.
  • Updates. DATUM checks for new versions and fetches only the files that changed, so a typical update is a few megabytes rather than another full install. Releases are signed, and an update that does not verify is refused.
  • 3Dconnexion SpaceMouse support, over raw HID or the 3DxWare driver, whichever is available.
